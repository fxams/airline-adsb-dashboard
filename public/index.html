Method and limitations
    
 
  • QTR vs UAE: every UTC day of adsb.lol globe_history daily dumps for 2025-11-04 to 2026-11-03 is streamed and filtered to A7- (QTR) and A6-E (UAE) registrations with QTR/UAE callsigns; only compact per-flight aggregates are kept. The 2026-05-06 dump does not exist. 
    • 
 
  • Mar to Jun 2026 dumps contain far fewer QTR/UAE flights than later months; whether that is ADS-B coverage or real operations is not verified here.
    • 
 
  • Fleet counts come from the tar1090 aircraft database; they may include stored aircraft and exclude leased or foreign-registered ones.
    • 
 
  • Other airlines: 3 sampled days, global; live view: 250 nm around six hub airports.
    • 
 
  • All figures are estimates. No delay, punctuality, fuel or safety figures are shown. Times are UTC.
