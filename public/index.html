Definitions, method and confidence

Limits: ADS-B receivers are sparse over oceans, Africa, Siberia and polar routes, so flights there may be partly or fully unseen. Airborne hours are observed hours only and are a lower bound (real utilisation is higher). Flights with no resolvable origin or destination are excluded from route and distance metrics (see "resolved %"). Some ends are inferred as the hub (see "inferred %"). Seat counts are typical, not actual. Fleet size comes from a public aircraft database and counts passenger and cargo airliners.

Method and limitations
    
 
  • QTR vs UAE: every UTC day of adsb.lol globe_history daily dumps for 2025-11-04 to 2026-11-03 is streamed and filtered to A7- (QTR) and A6-E (UAE) registrations with QTR/UAE callsigns; only compact per-flight aggregates are kept. The 2026-05-06 dump does not exist. 
    • 
 
  • Mar to Jun 2026 dumps contain far fewer QTR/UAE flights than later months; whether that is ADS-B coverage or real operations is not verified here.
    • 
 
  • Fleet counts come from the tar1090 aircraft database; they may include stored aircraft and exclude leased or foreign-registered ones.
    • 
 
  • Other airlines: 3 sampled days, global. Live view: 250 nm point tiles (API maximum) centred on Doha, Dubai and other busy hubs, then a slower global sweep. Not a per-type /v2/type loop.
    • 
 
  • City pairs: flights are stitched across coverage gaps (same aircraft and callsign, up to 16 h). When only one end is seen (e.g. the far end over the ocean or Australia/Asia had no receivers), the other end is set to the hub (DOH/DXB) and flagged “one end inferred”. Every pair is listed, including single flights, with a low-confidence flag (under 5 flights or mostly inferred).
    • 
 
  • Live selected-flight origin/destination is shown only when a connected source (adsbdb.com callsign lookup or QTR/UAE history) is geographically plausible against the displayed position: within a tolerance of the origin–destination great-circle corridor and along-track progress, with heading checked when well between the airports. Oceanic tracks, polar routings and SIDs are allowed; a pair that is far off that corridor (for example BUD–DOH while over Iceland) is not displayed. The panel then says Route unverified/unavailable and does not draw remaining route, progress or ETA from it. No replacement city pair is invented. An alternative source is used only if it is independently consistent (and labelled). Remaining-route weather intersections use the same gated pair.
    • 
 
  • Flown tracks (one-day overlay): real positions from one full UTC day of adsb.lol trace data, simplified with Douglas-Peucker (0.05°); breaks show no coverage. The white dashed line is the great circle for comparison.
    • 
 
  • 365-day airport tracks: every airport that appears as a QTR origin or destination in the 365-day globe_history aggregates () gets a lazy-loaded terminal-area tile. Geometry is clipped from sampled UTC days of adsb.lol traces (A7- / QTR and A6-E / UAE), Kalman/RTS-smoothed, assigned to an OurAirports runway end from the first/last on-runway heading and position (ambiguous tracks discarded), then clustered; the default map line is the medoid of the dominant cluster per runway and direction, with share of assigned flights. These are observed ADS-B paths, not published SID/STAR. Days with no dump (2026-05-06) or no receiver coverage are gaps, not zero traffic. Incremental rebuilds are an offline script (scripts/build-hist-tracks.py); Render free cannot download ~3.5 GB/day.
    • 
 
  • Published SID/STAR: each observed QTR airport is checked against that state’s public AIP/eAIP terms. DRAW only where reuse on a public website is explicitly allowed — today that is FAA CIFP (ARINC 424-18, U.S. government work, 17 U.S.C. § 105) for US airports, cycle . Lines are straight segments between published fixes, labelled with source, AIRAC cycle and “Not for navigation”, behind a zoom-in toggle. Heading-only legs are omitted rather than invented. Where terms forbid redistribution (NATS UK AIS T&Cs for EGLL and other UK aerodromes; DFS Germany; Airservices Australia; NAV CANADA; QCAA; and other national AIPs without an explicit grant), procedures are not redrawn; the airport panel links the official AIP AD 2 / AIS page. Not Navigraph, not Jeppesen, not EUROCONTROL EAD geometry. Nearest-published-procedure labels compare observed Kalman runway representatives to CIFP paths only at DRAW airports, and are omitted when nothing is close enough.
    • 
 
  • Per-flight SID/STAR identification: at US DRAW airports only, each stored Kalman departure/arrival clip (the hist subsample, not every 365-day flight) and a live selected ADS-B track in the terminal area is scored against CIFP procedures for that runway. Method: ordered published-fix hits within 2.0 nm, plus discrete Fréchet and DTW to the procedure path (resampled; Fréchet clipped to the last fix hit so later vectoring does not kill a flown SID). Confidence = 50% fix-fraction + 35% Fréchet + 15% DTW. Below 58%, or fewer than two ordered hits, the label is “No match / vectored”. Display: “Likely SID: NAME · N% match · estimated from ADS-B track, not the ATC clearance” in the aircraft detail panel, history-track popups, and per-runway share stats. Names and fixes come only from FAA CIFP — never fabricated. Licensing: matching still needs the procedure’s published fix sequence. FAA CIFP is a U.S. government work so names and fixes may be used. For non-US AIP (NATS EGLL and others) the coding tables were not ingested: NATS forbids redistributing AIS products; DFS/Airservices/NAV CANADA require permission; OurAirports navaids have coordinates but no SID/STAR association; ENR 4.4 waypoints likewise do not name a procedure; OpenAIP is CC BY-NC and is unused. Name-only identification is therefore off behind a clearly labelled toggle that shows this assessment only.
    • 
 
  • All figures are estimates. No delay, punctuality, fuel or safety figures are shown. Times are UTC.
    • 
 
  • En-route weather: NOAA/NWS Aviation Weather Center Data API (no key; server-side cache; max 100 req/min). International SIGMETs, US SIGMETs, and CONUS-only G-AIRMETs as hazard polygons labelled with the origin bulletin id; METARs (measured) and TAFs (forecasts) for a hub set plus selected origin/destination. Expired advisories are omitted. An upstream error is shown as unavailable, not as “no hazards”. Forecast winds aloft: NOAA GFS 1.0° pressure-level U/V via NOMADS grib filter (OpenDAP retired, SCN 25-81) on a global 0–359°E / 90S–90N grib-filter subset (the unfiltered 1° file is template 5.3 and is not decoded), decoded with a zero-dep simple-packing (template 5.0) reader and shown every 5°. World copies duplicate arrows across the antimeridian; Web Mercator clips above ~85° latitude. Not WAFS, not live turbulence. Not for operational flight planning. No satellite mosaic, no Open-Meteo.
    • 
 
  • Live ADS-B attribution (required by each source’s terms): aircraft positions from adsb.lol contributors, ODbL 1.0; adsb.fi open data (personal, non-commercial use; cite and link the homepage); airplanes.live when that API accepts this host (documented 1 req/s, 250 nm). Historical QTR/UAE aggregates remain adsb.lol globe_history (ODbL). Airports from OurAirports; live flight routes (origin/destination on click) from adsbdb.com, cross-checked with routes inferred from adsb.lol history; weather from NOAA/NWS Aviation Weather Center and origin agencies; basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO.
