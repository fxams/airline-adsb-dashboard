Efficiency index: what it does and does not mean

Index (0–100, 50 = typical) = 50% route directness + 30% share of airborne time within 1,000 ft of top altitude + 20% fewer descent level-offs, each a percentile within the same aircraft type. It is not fuel burn, delay, punctuality or safety. ATC, airspace, weather and winds drive much of it; small samples are low confidence. Mach, max speed, holding, go-arounds and taxi time are left out as unreliable.

Traced sample: full adsb.lol traces for about 160 aircraft, split into flights at gaps over 20 min; kept only flights that start and end below 4,000 ft within 15 nm of a scheduled airport and cover over 100 nm. Coverage is US and Europe heavy. Airline names: OpenFlights (ODbL). Airports: OurAirports (public domain). Land: Natural Earth (public domain).
